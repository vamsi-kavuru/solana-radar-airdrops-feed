Privacy Policy — Radar

Last updated: 2026-05-24

Radar is a Solana portfolio and airdrop tracker for mobile devices. This policy explains what data Radar handles and how.

Summary

  • Radar does not maintain a backend server that collects, stores, or syncs your data.
  • Wallet addresses you add are stored locally on your device (encrypted MMKV storage) and are never uploaded to any server we operate.
  • The app does make outbound network requests to public third-party services (Helius, Jupiter, GitHub Pages) to fetch on-chain data, token prices, and the airdrop catalog. These requests include public Solana addresses you've chosen to track — the same information that's already public on the blockchain.

What Radar collects

On your device only:

  • Wallet addresses you add (entered manually or connected via Mobile Wallet Adapter).
  • Airdrop subscription preferences (which airdrops you've toggled "Notify me" on).
  • Local notification scheduling state.

This data lives in MMKV storage on your device. It is never transmitted to servers we operate. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

What Radar sends to third-party services

To compute your portfolio, Radar's app makes direct API requests from your device to:

Service What's sent Why
Helius (helius.xyz) Wallet addresses (public Solana addresses) Token balances, native SOL balance, transaction history
GitHub Pages (vamsi-kavuru.github.io) None (read-only catalog fetch) Airdrop catalog refresh
Jupiter Worker (worker.jup.ag) Wallet addresses Airdrop eligibility checks

We do not have visibility into your usage of these services beyond what their own privacy policies describe. The addresses sent are already public on Solana's blockchain.

What Radar does NOT do

  • No account creation, sign-up, or login to Radar itself.
  • No analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or fingerprinting trackers.
  • No selling, sharing, or monetization of your data.
  • No collection of personally identifiable information (name, email, phone, device ID, location).

Permissions Radar requests

  • Internet — required to fetch on-chain data and the airdrop catalog.
  • Post notifications — required to send the local reminders you opt into for upcoming airdrops. You can revoke this at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app continues to work.

Children

Radar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has used the app, uninstalling it removes all locally-stored data.

Changes to this policy

We will update this document if Radar's data handling changes (for example, if a future version introduces a backend service). Material changes will be called out in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Open an issue at https://github.com/vamsi-kavuru/solana-radar-airdrops-feed or contact the publisher via the listing on the Solana dApp Store.
