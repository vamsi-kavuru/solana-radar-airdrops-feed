1. What Crypto Radar is

Crypto Radar is a free, read-only Solana portfolio and airdrop tracking app for mobile devices. It displays public on-chain data and a community-curated airdrop catalog. Crypto Radar does not custody assets, sign transactions on your behalf, or move funds.

2. Use at your own risk

  • The information Crypto Radar shows (balances, prices, airdrop eligibility, catalog entries) is sourced from third-party APIs and public on-chain data. It may be incomplete, delayed, or wrong.
  • The airdrop catalog is best-effort and includes entries from community sources. Inclusion is not an endorsement and does not confirm legitimacy. Always verify claim flows on the protocol's official site.
  • Eligibility readings (when shown) come from each protocol's public endpoint and may be inaccurate.
  • Cryptocurrency is volatile. Nothing in Crypto Radar is financial advice.

3. Scams and phishing

Be cautious of any airdrop that asks you to:

  • Share your seed phrase or private key.
  • Sign a transaction whose effect you don't understand.
  • Pay a "gas fee" upfront in exchange for a larger claim.

Crypto Radar does not enable any of the above. If a "claim" flow asks you to do any of these, it is almost certainly a scam — regardless of whether the airdrop appears in Crypto Radar's catalog.

4. Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA)

When you tap "Connect wallet" on Android, Crypto Radar uses the standard Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter protocol. Authorization happens inside the wallet app you choose (Phantom, Solflare, Backpack). Crypto Radar only receives the public address that wallet returns; it never sees your private key or seed phrase.
